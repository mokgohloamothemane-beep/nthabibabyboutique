## project overview
## organisation overwiew:
Nthabi's baby boutique is a fictional South African baby clothing and accessories store established in 2025. The business was created to provide parents and caretakers with high-quality, affordable, and stylish clothing for newborns, infants, and toddlers. The boutique understands that parents want comfortable, safe, durable, and fashionable clothing for their little ones. Therefore, it carefully selects products made from soft, baby-friendly fabrics that ensure comfort while maintaining modern designs. The store also provides seasonal collections and special occasion outfits.
## website goal
The website aims to establish a professional online presence for the boutique as ab rand a collective while making it easier for customers to browse through products and contact the business.
